It answers every call, day and night. It asks what the caller needs, books the job, and sends you the caller's name, number and reason. If a call is urgent, it sends it to your team or texts you.

What is the free mockup and report?

We design a homepage mockup for your business. We also call at busy times and count how many calls go unanswered. You get both for free, with no obligation.

Do I need both the website and the AI receptionist?

No. You can get the website only, or the AI receptionist only. The two are built to work together: the site brings people in, and the AI answers when they call.

Does the AI work with my calendar?

Yes. It books straight into Google Calendar and checks live availability, so there are no double bookings.
